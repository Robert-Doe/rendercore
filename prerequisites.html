PREREQUISITES Read once, referenced everywhere

The Foundational Ideas

The handful of primitives every module in this course assumes you already have a handle on — the way an OS course assumes you know what a register is before it explains a bootloader.

Every module's "00 Prerequisites" section links back to specific pages here instead of re-explaining these ideas inline. That's on purpose: a parser module shouldn't have to re-teach "what a tree is" every time it uses one. Read these eight pages once, then treat them as reference — skim back to one whenever a module's tag-link points at it.
